# Check Student Leave

**Monitor and Verify Student Leave Requests Conveniently**

The **Check Student Leave** menu in the eSchool Mobile application is used by teachers to monitor student absence/permission requests submitted by parents/guardians, as well as to manage student leave approvals.

## Access Rights & Approval Guidelines

To maintain data privacy and order, leave request access permissions are configured according to each respective class:

1. **Subject Teachers**
   * Only teachers who **teach in the student's class** can view leave requests submitted by parents/guardians.
   * Teachers who do not teach in that class (e.g., teachers from another department or teachers without scheduled classes in that room) **cannot view** the student's leave requests.
   * Subject teachers can only view the student's leave status (as information for attendance during class), and **cannot approve or reject** the leave request.

2. **Homeroom Teachers**
   * Have full authority to review and decide the status of student leave requests in their assigned class:
     * **Approve Leave**: Validate and approve the student's leave request.
     * **Reject Leave**: Reject the student's leave request if it is deemed inappropriate or does not meet requirements.
     * **Mandatory Reason for Rejection**: If a request is **rejected**, the homeroom teacher **must provide a clear reason for rejection** so parents/guardians understand why the leave was not approved.

---

## Key Features:

1. **Latest Leave Requests List**\
   Displays real-time absence/leave requests for students in the taught class.
2. **Detailed Leave Information**\
   Displays student name, class, leave date, leave type (sick, family event, etc.), as well as photo evidence/doctor's note if attached by parents.
3. **Rejection Reason Form**\
   A mandatory notes field that must be filled in by the homeroom teacher when rejecting a student leave request.

---

## Purpose and Benefits:

1. **More Practical & Paperless**: Student leave requests are processed directly through the app without physical letters.
2. **Instant Updates for Class Teachers**: Teachers currently in class immediately know which students are on leave for the day.
3. **Transparent for Parents**: Parents promptly receive notification of approval or rejection along with the reason.
