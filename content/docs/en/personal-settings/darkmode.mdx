## Switch Between Light and Dark Theme Easily

The **Dark Mode** feature allows teachers and staff to change the app’s appearance to a darker theme. This helps reduce eye strain, especially when using the app at night or in low-light environments.

<img src="/images/page/78_darkmode.jpg" alt="Light Mode" className="w-full max-w-sm max-h-100 object-contain mx-auto mb-6" />

### 1. How to Enable Dark Mode

1. Open the **eSchool Mobile - Guru & Staf** app.
2. Go to your **Profile**.
3. Tap **Tampilan Aplikasi** (App Appearance).
4. Select one of the available options:
   - **Terang** (Light)
   - **Gelap** (Dark)
   - **Ikuti Sistem** (Follow System)

### 2. Theme Options

- **Terang (Light)** — Default bright theme
- **Gelap (Dark)** — Dark background for better comfort in low light
- **Ikuti Sistem (Follow System)** — Automatically follows your device’s theme setting

### 3. Benefits of Using Dark Mode

- Reduces eye strain during night-time use
- Saves battery on devices with OLED screens
- Provides a more comfortable reading experience
- Creates a modern and professional look

### 4. Instant Application

Once the toggle is switched, the theme will change immediately across the entire application without needing to restart the app.
